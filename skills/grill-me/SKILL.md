name: grill-me
version: 1.0.0
description: Relentlessly interview the user one question at a time to stress-test an existing plan or design, committing to a recommended answer for each open decision and pushing back on weak ones. Use ONLY when the user explicitly asks to be challenged on a plan they already have — "grill me", "stress-test this", "poke holes in my plan", "pressure-test it", "play devil's advocate", "challenge my assumptions", "find the flaws". Do NOT use for ordinary "help me plan/design X" requests, which want collaboration, not interrogation.
argument-hint: "[plan, design, or file to grill me on]"
---

Interview me relentlessly about every aspect of this plan until we reach a shared understanding. Walk down each branch of the design tree, resolving dependencies between decisions one-by-one.

Ask the questions one at a time.

For each question: ask it first and wait for my answer. Then give your recommended answer and say where you agree or disagree. If my answer is weak or contradicts an earlier decision, push back and say why — don't just accept it and move on.

If a question can be answered by exploring the codebase, explore the codebase instead.

If no plan or design is already in our conversation, first ask me to describe it (or point you at the doc, issue, or file) before starting.
